RHIZOMORPH NESTING LAB

    WHAT TO FEEL FOR
    1. Open BLOG, then 2026. Can you read the path yolk, BLOG, 2026 from the lines alone, without the Path readout?
    2. With two levels open, switch the siblings between Stay, Fade and Leave. Which one keeps you oriented? In Leave, do you miss the way sideways to WORK?
    3. Go back three ways: Escape, pressing the open parent again, and the Left arrow. Which did you reach for first?
    4. Keyboard only: Tab to the yolk, then arrows. In a column, Right opens and Left closes or goes up. Enter selects.
    5. Set Top level to Column. It is now close to an ordinary tree view with good lines. Is that the rhizomorph, or has it become a file tree?

    Each item is a real button; an item with a further level says whether it is open. The lines are drawn on a canvas that assistive technology ignores. A production version would use nested lists.
