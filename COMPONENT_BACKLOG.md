## How to Add Entries

When running `/compound-engineering:eidotter-apply` or `/compound-engineering:eidotter-audit`, missing components are identified and added here.

**Entry format:**
```markdown
| ComponentName | ProjectA, ProjectB | Low/Medium/High | Backlog/In Progress/Done | Brief description |
```

**Complexity guide:**
- **Low**: Simple presentational component, <100 lines
- **Medium**: Stateful component or complex styling, 100-300 lines
- **High**: Complex state, accessibility concerns, >300 lines
