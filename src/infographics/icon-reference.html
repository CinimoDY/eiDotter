The Custom PixelX Story
pixelarticons v2 has no standalone X/close glyph. Rather than using their slashed-circle (Cancel), we hand-drew a pixel-art X mark matching their style: 24×24 viewBox, fill-based single path, 2×2 unit "pixels" on the grid. This is the only icon in eidotter not sourced from pixelarticons.
